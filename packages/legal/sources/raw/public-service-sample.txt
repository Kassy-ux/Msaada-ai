TITLE: Public Service Delivery and Citizen Rights (Kenya) — Selected Provisions

SECTION 1: RIGHT TO FAIR ADMINISTRATIVE ACTION
Every person has the right to administrative action that is expeditious, efficient,
lawful, reasonable, and procedurally fair. A person whose rights have been adversely
affected by administrative action has the right to be given written reasons for that
action.

SECTION 2: ACCESS TO INFORMATION
Every citizen has the right to access information held by the state or by another
person, where required for the exercise or protection of any right or fundamental
freedom, subject to limitations provided by law.

SECTION 3: COMPLAINTS AGAINST PUBLIC OFFICERS
A person who experiences maladministration, corruption, or abuse of office by a public
officer may file a complaint with the Commission on Administrative Justice (Office of
the Ombudsman) or the Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission, depending on the nature of
the complaint.

SECTION 4: DELAYS IN GOVERNMENT SERVICES
Where a government office unreasonably delays or denies a service a citizen is entitled
to, such as issuance of identification documents, land title processing, or business
licensing, the affected person may escalate the matter through the relevant ministry's
complaints channel or the Ombudsman.

SECTION 5: JUDICIAL REVIEW
A person aggrieved by an unlawful, irrational, or procedurally unfair decision of a
public body may apply to the High Court for judicial review, seeking orders to compel,
prohibit, or quash the decision.
