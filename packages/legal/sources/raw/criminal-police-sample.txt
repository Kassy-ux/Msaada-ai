TITLE: Criminal Procedure and Police Powers (Kenya) — Selected Provisions on Arrest, Assault, and Reporting

SECTION 1: RIGHT TO REPORT A CRIME
Any person who is a victim of or witness to a crime, including assault, theft, or violence,
has the right to report the matter to the nearest police station. The police are required
to record the complaint in the Occurrence Book (OB) and issue an OB number to the
complainant upon request.

SECTION 2: RIGHTS OF AN ARRESTED PERSON
A person who is arrested has the right to be informed promptly, in a language they
understand, of the reason for their arrest; the right to remain silent; the right to
communicate with an advocate and other persons whose assistance is necessary; and the
right to be brought before a court as soon as reasonably possible, but not later than
twenty-four hours after being arrested.

SECTION 3: MEDICAL EXAMINATION AFTER ASSAULT
A victim of physical or sexual assault is entitled to request a medical examination and a
P3 form from the police, which is used by a medical practitioner to document injuries for
use as evidence in criminal proceedings.

SECTION 4: PROTECTION AGAINST UNLAWFUL DETENTION
No person shall be subjected to arbitrary arrest or detention. Any person unlawfully
arrested or detained has the right to compensation and may petition the High Court for
enforcement of their rights.

SECTION 5: DUTY OF POLICE TO INVESTIGATE
The police service has a duty to investigate reported crimes, including assault and
violence, and to take appropriate action, which may include arrest of a suspect,
collection of evidence, and referral of the matter to the Office of the Director of
Public Prosecutions.
