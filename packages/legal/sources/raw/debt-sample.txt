TITLE: Debt Collection and Borrower Rights (Kenya) — Selected Provisions

SECTION 1: LAWFUL DEBT COLLECTION PRACTICES
A lender or debt collector must act lawfully and professionally when recovering a debt.
Harassment, threats, public shaming, contacting a borrower's employer or family members
without consent, or contacting a borrower at unreasonable hours are prohibited practices.

SECTION 2: DIGITAL LENDING AND DATA PROTECTION
Digital lending platforms are prohibited from accessing a borrower's phone contacts,
photos, or messages without explicit consent, and from using such data to shame or
coerce borrowers into repayment. Borrowers whose data has been misused may file a
complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

SECTION 3: RIGHT TO A CLEAR REPAYMENT AGREEMENT
Borrowers are entitled to receive clear terms of a loan agreement, including the
principal amount, interest rate, repayment schedule, and any penalties, before entering
into the agreement.

SECTION 4: CIVIL DEBT RECOVERY PROCESS
Recovery of a lawful debt must generally be pursued through civil court process, such as
the Small Claims Court for amounts within its jurisdiction, rather than through self-help
measures such as seizure of property without a court order.

SECTION 5: HARASSMENT AND DEFAMATION BY DEBT COLLECTORS
A borrower who has been defamed, harassed, or had their reputation damaged by a lender or
debt collection agency may have grounds for a civil claim for damages, in addition to
regulatory complaints against licensed lenders.
