TITLE: Consumer Protection (Kenya) — Selected Provisions

SECTION 1: RIGHT TO INFORMATION
Consumers have the right to accurate information regarding the quality, quantity, and
price of goods and services offered to them, and shall not be misled by false or
deceptive advertising.

SECTION 2: PROHIBITION OF UNFAIR TRADE PRACTICES
A supplier of goods or services shall not engage in unfair, deceptive, or unconscionable
practices, including selling defective goods without disclosure, false representation of
goods, or failure to honor warranties.

SECTION 3: RIGHT TO REDRESS
A consumer who has suffered loss as a result of defective goods or unfair trade practices
is entitled to seek redress, including repair, replacement, refund, or compensation from
the supplier or manufacturer.

SECTION 4: MOBILE MONEY AND DIGITAL TRANSACTION DISPUTES
A consumer who has been a victim of unauthorized mobile money transactions, fraud, or
billing errors may file a complaint with the service provider and, where unresolved,
escalate the matter to the Communications Authority of Kenya or the relevant financial
sector regulator.

SECTION 5: WHERE TO SEEK HELP
Consumers may lodge complaints with the Kenya Consumers Protection Advisory Committee,
the Competition Authority of Kenya, or pursue civil claims in the Small Claims Court for
matters involving smaller monetary amounts.
