TITLE: Gender-Based Violence (Kenya) — Selected Provisions on Protection and Support

SECTION 1: DEFINITION AND SCOPE
Gender-based violence includes physical, sexual, psychological, and economic harm
inflicted on a person, often based on their gender, and includes domestic violence,
sexual assault, defilement, and harmful cultural practices such as female genital
mutilation.

SECTION 2: RIGHT TO REPORT AND SEEK PROTECTION
A survivor of gender-based violence has the right to report the incident to the police
and request a P3 form for medical documentation. Survivors may also apply to court for a
protection order against the perpetrator.

SECTION 3: CONFIDENTIALITY AND SUPPORT SERVICES
Survivors of gender-based violence are entitled to confidential handling of their case by
authorities and access to support services, including counseling, medical care, and
shelter, often provided through gender desks at police stations and partner
organizations.

SECTION 4: SEXUAL OFFENCES
Sexual offences, including rape and defilement, are serious criminal offences under
Kenyan law. Survivors should seek immediate medical attention, ideally within 72 hours,
to preserve forensic evidence, and report the matter to the police.

SECTION 5: WHERE TO SEEK HELP
Survivors can seek help from the National GBV hotline, gender desks at police stations,
FIDA Kenya, and other legal aid and support organizations specializing in gender-based
violence cases.
