TITLE: Land Rights and Disputes (Kenya) — Selected Provisions

SECTION 1: PROTECTION OF LAND RIGHTS
Every person has the right to own property, including land, either individually or in
association with others, in any part of Kenya, subject to the Constitution and other
written law. Land may be held under freehold, leasehold, or customary tenure.

SECTION 2: UNLAWFUL EVICTION FROM LAND
No person shall be evicted from their land or have their access to land denied without
due process of law, including notice and an opportunity to be heard, except in
accordance with a court order or other lawful authority.

SECTION 3: RESOLUTION OF LAND DISPUTES
Disputes relating to land ownership, boundaries, succession, or unlawful occupation may
be filed with the Environment and Land Court, which has jurisdiction to hear and
determine such matters, or referred to alternative dispute resolution mechanisms
including land tribunals where applicable.

SECTION 4: LAND FRAUD AND ILLEGAL ALLOCATIONS
Any allocation of public land or transfer of land obtained through fraud, misrepresentation,
or corrupt practices is void and may be challenged in court. Affected parties should
preserve title documents, survey records, and any correspondence related to the
transaction.

SECTION 5: SUCCESSION AND INHERITANCE OF LAND
Where a landowner dies without a valid will, the land forms part of their estate and is
distributed among beneficiaries according to the Law of Succession Act, through the
process of obtaining letters of administration from the High Court or Magistrate's Court.
