TITLE: Employment Act, 2007 (Kenya) — Selected Provisions on Termination and Salary

SECTION 35: TERMINATION OF EMPLOYMENT
An employer shall not terminate a contract of employment unless there is a valid reason
connected to the employee's conduct, capacity, compatibility, or the operational
requirements of the employer. An employer must give the employee an opportunity to
respond to any allegations made against them before termination.

SECTION 17: WAGES AND SALARY PROTECTION
An employer must pay an employee's wages in full on or before the agreed pay day.
Failure to pay wages when due entitles the employee to claim the unpaid wages,
and may amount to a breach of contract enforceable before the Employment and
Labour Relations Court.

SECTION 41: NOTICE BEFORE TERMINATION
Before terminating employment on grounds of misconduct, poor performance, or physical
incapacity, an employer shall explain to the employee, in a language the employee
understands, the reason for which the employer is considering termination, and the
employee shall be entitled to have another employee or a shop floor union
representative present during the explanation.

SECTION 49: REMEDIES FOR UNFAIR TERMINATION
Where a labour officer or the Employment and Labour Relations Court finds that a
termination was unfair, remedies may include reinstatement, re-engagement, or
compensation not exceeding twelve months' wages.
