TITLE: Landlord and Tenant Rights (Kenya) — Selected Provisions on Lockouts and Rent Disputes

SECTION 1: PROHIBITION OF SELF-HELP EVICTION
A landlord shall not forcibly evict a tenant, lock a tenant out of the premises, disconnect
utilities, or remove a tenant's property without first obtaining a valid court order or
following the lawful eviction procedure. Self-help evictions, including changing locks or
barring entry without due process, are prohibited regardless of unpaid rent.

SECTION 2: PROCEDURE FOR RENT ARREARS
Where a tenant is in arrears, the landlord must issue a written notice specifying the
amount owed and a reasonable period to remedy the default before pursuing termination
of the tenancy through the appropriate tribunal, such as the Business Premises Rent
Tribunal or the Rent Restriction Tribunal, depending on the nature of the premises.

SECTION 3: TENANT REMEDIES FOR UNLAWFUL LOCKOUT
A tenant who has been unlawfully locked out may apply for an urgent court order for
reinstatement of possession, and may also be entitled to compensation for losses
suffered as a result of the unlawful lockout, including damage to property left inside
the premises.

SECTION 4: NOTICE PERIODS
Termination of a periodic tenancy generally requires notice consistent with the rental
period (e.g. one month's notice for a monthly tenancy), unless the tenancy agreement
specifies otherwise, and subject to any applicable statutory minimums.
